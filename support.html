Help and support

Questions, problems, or ideas? We read every message.

Contact us

Email brightdaysapp@gmail.com. Bright Days is made by one person, so it may take a day or two to hear back.

If something isn't working, it helps to include your phone model, what you were doing, and a screenshot if you can. Please don't send journal entries or other private details unless you want to.

Need help right now?

We can't respond to emergencies by email. If you're in the US:

  • In crisis: call or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, any time.
  • Treatment and support referrals: SAMHSA National Helpline, 1 800 662 4357. Free, confidential, 24/7.
  • In danger: call 911.

Common questions

Where is my information stored?

Only on your phone. There are no accounts and no Bright Days server holding your data. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I move to a new phone?

With Bright Days Plus, go to Settings → Backup and restore → Back up now and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, like iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Files, or email. On the new phone, install Bright Days, tap Restore from a backup on the welcome screen, and pick the file. Restoring is always free.

Backups include your journal and photos and aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

I changed my sobriety date. Is that okay?

Yes. Go to Settings → Your details. Your counter, milestones, and badges update right away. Every day counts, and so does starting again.

What's included in Bright Days Plus?

Craving support, daily check ins, milestones, meeting search, your journal, reasons, support circle, and gratitude are always free. Plus adds insights and charts, the monthly summary, extra counters, journal prompt packs, search, and PDF export, your own milestones and share cards, meeting stats and attendance PDFs, and backups. It's available monthly, yearly, or as a one time lifetime purchase.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Bright Days. On Android, open the Play Store, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

I already paid but Plus isn't showing.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID (or Google account) you bought with, then open Settings → Bright Days Plus and tap Restore purchases. For refunds, Apple handles requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A meeting listing is wrong.

Meeting listings come from the local AA and NA offices that publish them, so they're the ones who can fix them. Open the meeting in Bright Days to see who lists it, or check with your local intergroup or area. Meetings change often, so it's always worth confirming before you go.

Meeting search can't find my location.

Bright Days only uses your location while you search. If you said no to location access, type a city or ZIP code instead, or turn it on in your phone's Settings → Bright Days → Location.

My reminders aren't showing up.

Check that notifications are allowed for Bright Days in your phone's settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them. Then turn the reminder off and on again in Settings inside the app.

I don't want anyone to see what this app is.

Turn on Lock the app to require Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode, and Discreet reminders so notifications just say "You have a reminder." Both are in Settings → Privacy.

Is Bright Days connected to AA or NA?

No. Bright Days isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Alcoholics Anonymous, Narcotics Anonymous, SMART Recovery, Recovery Dharma, or any other organization. It works alongside whatever path you're on.

Can Bright Days replace treatment?

No. It's a self help companion, not medical or psychological care. It works best alongside the people and programs that support you.
