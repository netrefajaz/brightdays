Nothing in the app is a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Talk with a qualified professional about your own health and recovery.

Your health and safety come first

Bright Days can't monitor you, can't contact anyone for you, and can't respond to emergencies. If you're in danger, think you may have overdosed, or are having thoughts of harming yourself, call 911 or 988 right away.

Withdrawal from some substances, including alcohol, benzodiazepines, and opioids, can cause serious medical problems. Bright Days doesn't give medical guidance about detox or withdrawal. Please talk with a doctor or a treatment program about the safest way for you to stop or cut back.

The craving tools, breathing and grounding exercises, and plans are meant to help in hard moments. They may not work for everyone or every time, and they're not a substitute for professional care.
