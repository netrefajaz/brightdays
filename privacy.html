Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version

  • Everything you add to Bright Days is stored on your phone. There are no accounts, and we don't have a server that holds your data.
  • We don't use analytics, ads, trackers, or data brokers, and we never sell or share your information.
  • The app only goes online when you search for meetings or open a link, and only sends what that search needs.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything in it.

Bright Days is a sobriety companion app made by Jasmine Netrefa-Drinkwater ("we," "us"). Recovery is personal, and this policy explains plainly what the app does with your information. If anything here is unclear, email brightdaysapp@gmail.com.

When the app goes online

Bright Days works offline. It only connects to the internet in these cases:

Meeting search

When you search for AA or NA meetings, the app needs a location. It uses your phone's location if you allow it, or a city or ZIP code you type.

  • NA meetings: your location, rounded to within about a mile (or several miles for online meetings), the day you picked, and your search radius are sent to the BMLT meeting list (aggregator.bmltenabled.org), which is run by NA volunteers.
  • AA meetings: the app downloads public meeting lists from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) for the general area around you. Your exact location isn't sent. Only the area being downloaded, roughly 30 miles across, can be inferred from the request.
  • Typing a city or ZIP code: your phone's built in geocoding service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) turns it into map coordinates.

Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your phone connects. Recent search results are kept on your phone for up to a day so the same search doesn't have to be repeated. Your location is never saved by Bright Days.

Links you open

Tapping things like "Get the link," "More info," official meeting finders, directions, or a call or text button hands you off to your browser, maps, phone, or messages app. What happens there is covered by those services' own policies.

Purchases

If you buy Bright Days Plus, payment is handled entirely by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your name, email, or payment details. To know which features to unlock, the app checks your purchase through the App Store and a subscription service we use to manage purchases (RevenueCat). That service receives an anonymous ID created by the app and your purchase history for Bright Days, and nothing else from the app.

Phone permissions

  • Location: only while you're using meeting search. Never in the background. You can say no and type a city or ZIP code instead.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent from a server. "Discreet reminders" hides anything about recovery on your lock screen.
  • Photos: only when you add a photo to a reason. The app sees only the photo you pick.
  • Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode: only if you turn on the app lock. Your phone checks it; Bright Days never receives your face, fingerprint, or passcode.

Sharing, exports, and backups

Some features create a file or image: milestone share cards, journal and meeting PDFs, the monthly summary, and backups. These are only created when you tap the button, and they go wherever you send them through your phone's share sheet (for example Files, iCloud Drive, email, or a message).

Backups contain everything in the app, including your journal and photos, and they are not encrypted. Keep backup files somewhere private, and only share them with people you trust.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign ups, or logins.
  • No analytics, crash tracking, advertising, or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No AI or automated review of your journal or check ins. Insights are simple counts and patterns worked out on your phone.

Deleting your information

Because your information only lives on your phone, you're in control. Delete individual entries in the app, or delete Bright Days from your phone to erase everything. If you made backup files, delete those wherever you saved them. We have no copy to delete.

Age

Bright Days is for adults 18 and older (see the Terms of Use). We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical care

Bright Days is a self help tool (see the Terms of Use). It isn't medical, psychological, or emergency care, and it doesn't replace a doctor, therapist, sponsor, or treatment program. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988, or call 911 if you're in danger.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Bright Days handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the app's update notes.
